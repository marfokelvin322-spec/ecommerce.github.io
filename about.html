Our Story

Built for Ghana's Tech Lovers

KelStore was founded with a simple mission: to give Ghanaians access to the latest and greatest in technology at honest prices — with zero compromise on quality.

We started as a small operation in Accra and have grown into a trusted name across Ghana. From the newest iPhone to the latest smartwatches and laptops, every product we sell is 100% genuine and backed by our quality guarantee.

Our mentor organisation is Telefonika — one of Europe's most respected online electronics retailers — whose customer-first approach, transparent pricing, and logistics excellence inspire how we operate every day.

Accessibility

Our Accessibility Commitment

KelStore is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities.

♿ Accessibility Features Implemented

  • 1
    Skip to Content Link

    Keyboard users can bypass the navigation and jump directly to main content using the "Skip to main content" link that appears on focus.

  • 2
    ARIA Labels & Roles

    All interactive elements, images, and sections have descriptive ARIA labels for screen reader compatibility.

  • 3
    High Contrast Support

    The site automatically adapts to high contrast mode using CSS prefers-contrast media query.

  • 4
    Reduced Motion Support

    Animations are disabled for users who have enabled prefers-reduced-motion in their system settings.

📋 Standards We Follow

WCAG 2.1 Level AA — We aim to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines for all key pages.
Semantic HTML5 — Proper use of headings, landmarks, lists, and form labels.
Keyboard Navigation — Full keyboard navigability throughout the site with visible focus indicators.

Report an Issue

If you experience any accessibility barriers on our site, please contact us at our contact page. We commit to addressing issues within 48 hours.
